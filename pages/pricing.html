Frequently Asked Questions

How is my payment processed?

Payments are processed securely via Cashfree Payments, an RBI-licensed payment aggregator. We do not store your card, CVV, or banking credentials.

When does my license activate?

Activation is instant. As soon as your UPI or card payment succeeds, your Master Journal desktop application detects the active license and unlocks all Pro features.

Will my trades remain private?

Yes, absolutely. Master Journal is a 100% local-first software. Your journal entries and trade data remain stored locally on your PC and your personal Google Drive account.
