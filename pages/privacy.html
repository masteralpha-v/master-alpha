Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 2026 • Master Alpha Ltd.

1. Introduction & Overview

Master Alpha Ltd. ("Master Alpha", "we", "us", or "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy describes our privacy practices regarding information collected through our official website and our desktop applications, including Master Journal.

Our core architecture is local-first and privacy-by-design. Your financial trade data, journal records, strategies, and notes remain under your exclusive control at all times.

2. Google OAuth & User Data Access

Master Journal provides an optional feature allowing users to backup their trading journal database to their personal Google Drive storage. When you choose to sign in with Google within Master Journal, we request the following specific scopes:

  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/drive.appdata (Application Data Folder): This scope allows Master Journal to create, read, update, and restore backup archive files strictly within the hidden, sandboxed Application Data folder in your Google Drive. Master Journal does NOT have access to any other files, folders, documents, photos, or data stored in your Google Drive.
  • https://www.googleapis.com/auth/userinfo.email & profile: Allows Master Journal to identify your active Google account email and avatar locally in the application's account settings.

3. Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance (Limited Use Disclosure)

Master Journal's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Specifically, we adhere to the following strict principles:

  • No Human Reading: No human, employee, contractor, or developer at Master Alpha ever has access to, inspects, or reads your Google Drive backup files.
  • No Third-Party Advertising: We never use or transfer Google user data to serve advertisements, retarget users, or sell to data brokers.
  • No AI Model Training: We do not use user data obtained via Google APIs to train generalized artificial intelligence or machine learning models.
  • Direct Transmission: Backup data flows directly between your desktop client and Google's official Drive APIs (www.googleapis.com). It does not pass through any intermediary proprietary server operated by Master Alpha.

4. Data Storage, Retention & Deletion

Because your data is stored locally on your machine and inside your personal Google Drive account:

  • Local Storage: You can completely purge your local data by clearing the application database or uninstalling Master Journal.
  • Cloud Backups: You can delete any or all backup files directly from within the Master Journal Backup & Restore settings interface, or via your Google Account Management dashboard.
  • Revoking Access: You can revoke Master Journal's authorization at any time by visiting Google Account Security Permissions.

5. Security Measures

We employ industry-standard security protocols to safeguard your authorization credentials. OAuth tokens (including refresh tokens) are stored locally in restricted-access system files (user-session.json) with 0600 file permissions and are communicated exclusively over TLS-encrypted connections.
