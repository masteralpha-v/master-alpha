You may cancel your subscription or stop renewing at any time. When you cancel a subscription, your Pro access will remain active until the end of your current paid billing period (e.g., 30 Days, 90 Days, 180 Days, or 365 Days). You will not be charged again after the current period expires.

3. Refund Policy

Because Master Journal is a downloadable digital product that can be tested, we offer clear and fair refund guidelines:

However, exceptional requests submitted within 48 hours of initial purchase will be evaluated on a case-by-case basis.
